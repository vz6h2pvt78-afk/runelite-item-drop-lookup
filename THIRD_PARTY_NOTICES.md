Runtime resources under src/main/resources/:

- Night beast drop context adapts revision 15373912; the exact Zulrah/Dragonstone
  addition follows Zulrah 15385964, Template:RareDropTable 15104778 and
  Module:RareDropLines 15343549. The rational rate is per roll, with two rolls
  per kill. Their offline evidence fixtures in tools/fixtures/ retain the Wiki
  attribution and license above; no location association is inferred.

- acquisition_events.json: reviewed harvest actions, requirements, location yields
  and ordinary loot/capture routes from Ent (15388968), Dragon impling (15371528),
  Dragon impling jar (15371529), and Impling (15360701). Each association includes
  revision links. The full ordinary loot table is retained separately from the
  subset of outcomes currently exposed in item searches. Offline evidence text in
  tools/fixtures/acquisition_event_evidence.json retains the same Wiki license.
  This fixture also retains Template:Ent Calculator (14807076) and
  Module:Ent calculator (14807078). The ordinary-Logs level-45 disagreement is
  documented in tools/fixtures/ent_boundary_conflict.json without resolving the
  Wiki sources or translating their calculation into gameplay probabilities.

- wiki_ground_sources.json: twelve reviewed ground-pickup rows adapted from
  Spade (revision 15387831), Knife (15350068), Bucket (15390041), Snape grass
  (15225056), and Red spiders' eggs (15183987). docs/data/ground_sources.json
  preserves page/history URLs, revisions, exact templates and adaptation evidence.
  tools/fixtures/ground_spawns.json preserves source wikitext and Small fishing
  net (15384438) as an excluded-method regression fixture. These Wiki-derived
  fixtures, manifest and data retain the attribution and Wiki license above.

- wiki_monster_drops.json: extracted monster drop tables. The monsterName field
  records the source Wiki page title. Exact original revisions were not retained.
- wiki_shop_sources.json: extracted shop tables and shop infobox text. sourceName
  records the source page title. Exact original revisions were not retained.
- monster_locations.json: extracted location/access text and reviewed drop
  associations. Each location retains sourceUrl, sourcePage and revisionId;
  docs/data/monster_locations_report.json contains the revision manifest.
- item_packs.json: reviewed pack contents and selected costs. packName identifies
  the Wiki page; wikiRevision and verifiedOn identify the checked version/date.
- potion_families.json: curated names from the category and linked potion pages:
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Category:Potions .
- item_sources.json: curated reward facts and notes from Rune pouch, Slayer
  Rewards, Justine's stuff for the Last Shopper Standing, Mage Training Arena
  Rewards, Grace and Graceful hood Wiki pages.

The bundled data_sources.txt supplies source page URLs and links to contributor
histories. These are attribution links; they do not imply that current page
contents are identical to older snapshots. Offline diagnostics in tools/data/
and docs/data/monster_locations_report.json retain the same source attribution
for any extracted Wiki material. tools/data/monster_location_overrides.json
records the source URLs for reviewed classifications and adapted notes.
