Monster portraits
Game artwork copyright Jagex Ltd. Obtained from OSRS Wiki contributors on 2026-11-05.
These images are game media, not covered by the Wiki text license or this project's BSD license.
Each runtime portrait is an aspect-preserving 36 by 40 transparent PNG.
A portrait illustrates a reviewed form of the named source or an explicitly reviewed group;
it does not describe every visual variant, drop, location, level, or requirement.
No artwork is downloaded during plugin use.
Individual file-page links, original image hashes, thumbnail URLs, local hashes,
own-page revision evidence, representative choices and held cases are recorded in
tools/data/monster_artwork_review.json.
The retained original goblin.png is the accepted S43 reference, SHA-256
334d2b27eacedfad4e946c6651eaaf981ea1e0697c7371a04e11ec0ae696fe2f;
the runtime Goblin portrait preserves those pixels with transparent centering.
