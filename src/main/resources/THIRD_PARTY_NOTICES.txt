Runtime resources under src/main/resources/:

- impling_loot.json presents the reviewed jar outcomes and capture routes from the individual OSRS Wiki impling articles. Its evidence records link the selected revisions.
- cooking_recipes.json adapts the cooking-range table and the successful and burnt outcomes from Shrimps revision 15183319, with Raw shrimps revision 15331265 as supporting evidence. Permanent links are attached to the recipe. The alternate fire mention comes from the article prose.
- production_recipes.json and wiki_ground_additions.json select the normal furnace recipe and three Bronze bar pickups from https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/15315107 .
- shop_locations.json links offers to the listed Forestry and Slayer shop branches. Two Slayer stock entries also record their ammunition identity, purchase quantity and full price. https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/15374369 and https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/15373095 .
- Kamil’s Chocolate cake encounter qualification uses https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Special:PermanentLink/15200663 .
- animator_rewards.json records the animator preparation and three stated token awards from each of seven Animated Armour pages. Each entry links the applicable Wiki revision.
- ocean_bounty_access.json adapts the Eagle ray, Butterfly ray, Stingray, Narwhal and Orca articles. Each fact links its source revision. Named drop conditions are matched to their recorded quantity, rate and notes.
- shark_bounty_access.json records the bounty eligibility and jaw/liver conditions from Bull shark (15372302), Hammerhead shark (15285222) and Tiger shark (15379177). Each fact links its source revision.
- reviewed_slayer_access.json distinguishes Slayer kill levels, attack conditions, protection and finishing equipment for exact named monsters. Each fact carries the contributor page and permanent revision link.


- Night beast drop context adapts revision 15373912; the exact Zulrah/Dragonstone
  addition follows Zulrah 15385964, Template:RareDropTable 15104778 and
  Module:RareDropLines 15343549. The rational rate is per roll, with two rolls
  per kill. Their offline evidence fixtures in tools/fixtures/ retain the Wiki
  attribution and license above; no location association is inferred.

- acquisition_events.json: reviewed harvest actions, requirements, location yields
  and ordinary loot/capture routes from Ent (15388968), Dragon impling (15371528),
  Dragon impling jar (15371529), and Impling (15360701). Each association includes
  revision links. The full ordinary loot table is retained separately from the
  subset of outcomes currently exposed in item searches. Offline evidence text in
  tools/fixtures/acquisition_event_evidence.json retains the same Wiki license.

- wiki_gathering_sources.json: bounded ordinary Gathering adaptations from Iron ore
  (15182636), Iron rocks (15372456), South-west Varrock mine (15127886), Raw shrimps
  (15331265), Fishing spot (small net, bait) (15288182), Logs (15322883), and Tree
  (15226767), by OSRS Wiki contributors. Selected actions, requirements and named
  locations retain the applicable Wiki license above. Source/revision URLs are in
  data_sources.txt; separate offline fixtures/manifests retain the underlying text.

- wiki_ground_sources.json: twelve reviewed ground-pickup rows adapted from
  Spade (revision 15387831), Knife (15350068), Bucket (15390041), Snape grass
  (15225056), and Red spiders' eggs (15183987), by OSRS Wiki contributors.
  Selected locations, normalized text, and explicit membership/point counts
  retain CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 as described above. Source and history URLs are in
  data_sources.txt; the source repository's docs/data/ground_sources.json retains
  exact templates and revisions. Pickup quantities and access are not inferred.

- wiki_monster_drops.json: extracted monster drop tables. The monsterName field
  records the source Wiki page title. Exact original revisions were not retained.
- wiki_shop_sources.json: extracted shop tables and shop infobox text. sourceName
  records the source page title. Exact original revisions were not retained.
- monster_locations.json: extracted location/access text and reviewed drop
  associations. Each location retains sourceUrl, sourcePage and revisionId;
  docs/data/monster_locations_report.json contains the revision manifest.
- item_packs.json: reviewed pack contents and selected costs. packName identifies
  the Wiki page; wikiRevision and verifiedOn identify the checked version/date.
- potion_families.json: curated names from the category and linked potion pages:
  https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Category:Potions .
- item_sources.json: curated reward facts and notes from Rune pouch, Slayer
  Rewards, Justine's stuff for the Last Shopper Standing, Mage Training Arena
  Rewards, Grace and Graceful hood Wiki pages.

The bundled data_sources.txt supplies source page URLs and links to contributor
histories. These are attribution links; they do not imply that current page
contents are identical to older snapshots. Offline diagnostics in tools/data/
and docs/data/monster_locations_report.json retain the same source attribution
for any extracted Wiki material. tools/data/monster_location_overrides.json
records the source URLs for reviewed classifications and adapted notes.

## Reviewed access requirements

The overnight development access_requirements.json sidecar contains adapted
factual summaries by Item Drop Lookup from OSRS Wiki contributors, under
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Each fact includes its exact source page and revision URL.
Changes include paraphrasing and explicit monster, location, item and activity
scope. It is an incomplete reviewed sample, not a claim of universal coverage.
See docs/access-requirements.md and the per-fact evidence for provenance.

## Reviewed shared herbs

The 44 shared-herb additions in wiki_monster_drops.json and their appended
monster_locations.json bindings are adapted from OSRS Wiki contributors under
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. tools/fixtures/shared_herbs.json preserves attributed source
wikitext and extracted rendered rows. docs/data/shared_herb_expansion.json gives
the exact monster/template revision chain, source URLs and hashes for every row.
Changes include rational rate calculation, rounded display checks, members-world
conditions and explicit table/location associations. See docs/shared-herbs.md.
